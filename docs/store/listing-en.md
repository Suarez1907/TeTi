# Store listing — English

**App name (30):** TeTi: IPTV Player & EPG

**Short description (80):** Fast, ad-free IPTV player for your own M3U and Xtream playlists.

**Full description:**

TeTi is a modern, fast IPTV player for watching the playlists from your own provider.

⚠️ TeTi does not provide or include any channels, movies or other content. You need an M3U
link, M3U file or Xtream Codes account from a legal provider.

Highlights
• Xtream Codes, M3U link and local M3U file support
• 7-day grid TV guide (EPG) with time offset and automatic updates
• Catch-up for past programmes when your provider supports it
• Multiview: up to 4 channels at once
• Continue watching; synopses, ratings and cast for movies and series
• One-handed gestures: swipe for the channel list, brightness and volume; double tap to seek
• Parental lock, favorites, backup and restore
• Android TV with remote support, Chromecast
• No ads, no tracking

Movie and series data from TMDB. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or
certified by TMDB.
